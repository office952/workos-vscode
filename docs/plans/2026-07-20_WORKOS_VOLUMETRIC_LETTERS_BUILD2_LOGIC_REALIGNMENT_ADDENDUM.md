## Why GO is suspended

Owner discussion showed the central premise was incomplete:

- a constructive group is **not** obligatorily equal to one SVG layer;
- files may be prepared canonically by **layer** or by **color**;
- labels such as Maria, Ioana, Soare, Georgeta are **observed fixture/test labels only**, not domain concepts or permanent identity;
- the parser must not turn observations directly into Product Truth;
- a future assistant proposes and asks; the operator confirms; only confirmed truth is saved.

Until grouping mode + join semantics are re-audited, Build 2 UI units (chips, orphan UX, LED C, U1–U6) must not proceed.
